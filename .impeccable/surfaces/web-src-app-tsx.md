# Conta — surface brief

## Scope

Single responsive shell with three views: Início, Ferramentas, Atividade. Mode: Operate. Mobile-first with a desktop rail.

## Audience and job

Developers and stakeholders demoing the WebView bridge, and ordinary users encountering a familiar daily-finance app. The job is to read the month's money state, launch a few device-backed actions, and inspect the resulting protocol activity without leaving the product.

## Content and constraints

Synthetic account data (balance, transactions, categories), real bridge states, browser mock mode, native mode, loading/error/empty states. Camera, GPS, haptics, clipboard, notes, counter, device info, native UI, link, and events must all remain reachable. No account, backend, or real financial data exists.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Caderneta digital: deep ink-blue cover, cool paper pages, red stamp ink, ruled margin, green income. Standard app navigation and controls; the world enters through type, palette, density, and one signature move. The move is the running balance held in the transaction margin, with a live red stamp and a ruled activity detail that makes the bridge legible as a paper trail.
